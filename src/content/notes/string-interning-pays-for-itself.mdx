---
title: "String interning pays for itself"
description: "Identifier comparison becomes an integer compare."
pubDate: "Apr 06 2026"
tags:
  - compilers
  - performance
  - rust
---

Intern every identifier you find at lex time and pass `Symbol(u32)` around instead
of `String`.

Name resolution then compares integers instead of bytes, symbol tables have `u32` keys, and the AST stops holding thousands of tiny heap allocations.
It's a relatively cheap win in a compiler front end.
