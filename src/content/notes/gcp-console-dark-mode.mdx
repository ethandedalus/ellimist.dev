---
title: "Enabling Dark Mode in the GCP Console"
description: "How to enable the hidden dark mode in the Google Cloud Console"
pubDate: "August 6 2026"
tags:
  - gcp
---

If the blinding white of the GCP console is burning your eyes, open up devtools and paste the following lines of javascript into the console and execute them:

```js
document.body.setAttribute("data-cm-color-scheme", "dark");
document.body.setAttribute("data-cm-dark-launched", "true");
```

This will fix most of the problems. At least for now. Hopefully they update the console to have a toggle natively.
